We develop a lossy compression method for incompressible velocity fields that analytically preserves zero divergence and, on periodic domains, periodicity at every compression tolerance. The method represents velocities using property-preserving matrix-valued kernels and compresses their coefficients with standard lossy compressors. We develop low-rank, multilevel, and redundant kernel representations, derive bounds linking coefficient compression errors to velocity errors, and evaluate accuracy and storage on cavity, Taylor-Green, and species-transport flows.
